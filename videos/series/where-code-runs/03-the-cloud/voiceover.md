## [0:00-0:05] Hook
The cloud. It sounds like magic. It is not.

## [0:05-0:15] Definition
The cloud is just someone else's computers, in a data center, that you rent by the hour. Bare metal, virtual machines, or containers, all of it, rented.

## [0:15-0:36] Levels
How much you rent decides how much you manage. Infrastructure as a service, or IaaS, rents you the machine. Platform as a service, PaaS, rents the platform, so you only bring code. Software as a service, SaaS, rents the whole app.

## [0:36-0:54] Recap
So, to recap. Bare metal, you own the box. A virtual machine is a slice of a box. A container is a slice of an operating system. And the cloud is how you rent any of those.

## [0:54-1:06] Why
Why rent? You pay for what you use, scale up in minutes, and skip racking servers. The cost: you trust someone else with your data, and the bill can surprise you.
