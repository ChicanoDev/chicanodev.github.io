> On screen: `head`, `tail`, `tail -f`, `diff`.

## [0:00-0:05] Hook
A log file with two hundred lines. You do not need to read all of it. Here is how.

## [0:05-0:15] Head
Head shows the start of a file. By default ten lines, or ask for exactly how many with dash and a number.

## [0:15-0:27] Tail
Tail shows the end of a file. The most recent log lines are at the bottom, so tail is the one you will use most.

## [0:27-0:43] Follow
Add dash f and tail follows the file, printing new lines the instant they are written. This is how you watch a live log. Press control c to stop.

## [0:43-0:57] Diff
Diff compares two files. The less than line is from the first file, the greater than line is from the second. It points straight at what changed.

## [0:57-1:04] Less
And for big files you want to scroll through, use less. I have a short on that one.
