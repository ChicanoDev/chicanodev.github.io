# About ChicanoDev

ChicanoDev is a place to share practical knowledge about Linux, programming, DevOps, infrastructure, and AI automation.

I started this project because I believe experience and knowledge should be shared, not hoarded. I've been fortunate to spend decades working with computers, and I want to help others benefit from the things I've learned along the way.

ChicanoDev is especially dedicated to helping Chicanos, Latinx people, LGBTQIA+ folks, and others who have historically been underrepresented in technology. But everyone who's here to learn is welcome.

## Everyone belongs here

ChicanoDev is a welcoming space for people of all backgrounds, identities, and experience levels. Racism, sexism, homophobia, and transphobia have no place here.

Whether you're writing your first shell script or managing production Kubernetes clusters, I hope you'll find something useful here.

**Built on experience. Shared with everyone.**
